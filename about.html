About

One roof for learning cybersecurity.

Ask five people where to start in cybersecurity and you get five different answers. One says get Security+. Another says certs are useless and you should just do boxes. You end up with forty bookmarked links, accounts on half a dozen platforms, and no idea what to open first.

Our research put a number on it: stitching your own path across 6 to 8 disconnected platforms costs roughly $1,030 to $1,180 and 12 to 18 months of figuring out what to learn in what order. Content is not the problem. Nobody is short of content. The problem is the integration, and right now the student is the one doing it.

What The PenTrix does

01

Verified everything

Every resource carries a real URL, a real price, and a last-verified date. Dead links get pulled, prices get rechecked. If it is listed here, someone checked it.

02

Sequencing over catalog

A roadmap is not a link dump. Each path tells you what to do this week, what it builds on, and what it prepares you for. One trusted order, start to finish.

03

Cost transparency

For every goal, we show the cheapest verified path next to the mid-range and premium options, trade-offs included. No surprise subscription stacking.

04

Free core forever

The library, the roadmaps, and the cost data are free and stay free. That is not a launch offer. If we ever charge for anything, it will be something new on top, never the core you came for.

05

Crafted, not generated

Pages written and designed by people who care about how you learn, not filler to pad a sitemap. If a page does not help you learn, it does not ship.

Who it is for

The absolute beginner

You have a laptop, maybe just a phone, and no idea whether to start with networking, Linux, or a cert. You need one trusted starting point and someone honest about the order.

The career switcher

You have IT experience and want into a junior SOC or pentest role. You need to know which cert is actually worth your money and how to prove you can do the work in an interview.

The grinder

You are already deep into TryHackMe and Hack The Box, and you are hitting the gaps: paywalls, hint dependency, no feedback on your reports. You need structure that holds you to a real standard.

The founder

Built by Izaz

Izaz (The PenTrix) is a cybersecurity student, a bug bounty hunter recognized on YesWeHack, and a CTF player. He built The PenTrix because he got tired of answering “where do I start?” with a 40-link dump.

Free forever, for real

The library, the roadmaps, and the cost transparency are free forever. No trials, no paywalled basics, no “free” tier that quietly hides the good stuff.

What’s next

More paths are coming: deeper blue team tracks and a full digital forensics track, progress tracking that measures demonstrated skill instead of pages read, and community collections so learners can share paths that actually worked for them. These ship when they are ready, not on a fixed deadline.
