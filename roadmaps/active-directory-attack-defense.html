Roadmap

Active Directory Attack & Defense

Break a full AD lab with the same attacks used in real breaches, then learn to detect and stop each one.

Audience
Pentesters who can already scan and exploit a network and want the domain-admin playbook
Difficulty
Intermediate to Advanced
Length
12-18 weeks
Steps
8 steps

The sequence

0 of 8 steps

Tick a step when you finish it. Progress is saved in this browser only.

  1. 01
    10 h

    Why this comes next: AD attacks abuse normal features, not software bugs. You need domains, trusts, Kerberos, and LDAP in your head before any attack makes sense.

    • ADSecurity.orgguide · Intermediate · Free↗
    • HackTricksCheatsheet · Intermediate · Free↗
    • TryHackMeInteractive Lab · Beginner · Freemium↗
  2. 02
    12 h

    Why this comes next: AD is learned by doing, and you need a domain you are allowed to break. GOAD builds a full multi-domain forest on your machine so every step below has a target.

    • GOAD (Game of Active Directory)interactive lab · Intermediate · Free↗
    • HackTheBox AcademyInteractive Lab · Beginner · Freemium↗
    • ImpacketTool · Intermediate · Free↗
  3. 03
    10 h

    Why this comes next: Attackers do not guess paths to Domain Admin, they graph them. Learn collection and query basics now, because every attack later starts from what enumeration reveals.

    • BloodHoundTool · Intermediate · Freemium↗
    • The Hacker Recipesguide · Intermediate · Free↗
    • ImpacketTool · Intermediate · Free↗
  4. 04
    10 h

    Why this comes next: This is how most internal tests start: catch credentials off the wire with Responder or test weak passwords with Kerbrute, before you ever touch a real exploit.

    • ResponderTool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • Kerbrutetool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • SecListsCheatsheet · Beginner · Free↗
  5. 05
    12 h

    Why this comes next: The two classic AD attacks. Rubeus requests service tickets and pre-auth-less AS-REPs directly, so you see exactly how Kerberos design choices become credential theft.

    • Rubeustool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • ImpacketTool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • The Hacker Recipesguide · Intermediate · Free↗
  6. 06
    12 h

    Why this comes next: Credentials become access. Dump secrets with Pypykatz, abuse certificate services with Certipy, and chain it all the way to Domain Admin in your lab.

    • Pypykatztool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • Certipytool · Advanced · Free↗
    • HackTricksCheatsheet · Intermediate · Free↗
  7. 07
    12 h

    Why this comes next: Each attack you ran left logs. Learn the Windows event IDs and Sigma rules behind them, and practice triage on realistic alert data so you can defend what you can break.

    • Sigma rules repositoryCheatsheet · Intermediate · Free↗
    • The DFIR ReportNewsletter · Intermediate · Free↗
    • Splunk BOTS (Boss of the SOC)CTF Platform · Beginner · Free↗
  8. 08
    15 h

    Why this comes next: Offense teaches the attacks, hardening removes them: tiering, credential hygiene, and patching. CRTO is the hands-on cert that proves you can do both sides at exam depth.

    • Zero-Point Security CRTOCertification · Advanced · Paid↗
    • ADSecurity.orgguide · Intermediate · Free↗
    • Blue Team Labs OnlineInteractive Lab · Beginner · Freemium↗
