Roadmap

Security Certifications Guide

Which cert for which job, what each one actually proves, and what it costs in 2026.

Audience
Students deciding which security certifications are worth their time and money
Difficulty
Beginner to Advanced
Length
48-72 weeks
Steps
8 steps

The sequence

0 of 8 steps

Tick a step when you finish it. Progress is saved in this browser only.

  1. 01
    3 h

    Why this comes next: Certs cost real money, from about 200 to 1700 dollars, and HR listings name names. The roadmap shows where every major cert sits, and CEH is listed here because you will see it in job posts and should know what it actually signals before paying for it.

    • Security Certification Roadmap (Paul Jerimy)roadmap · Beginner · Free↗
    • EC-Council CEH v13Certification · Beginner · Paid↗
  2. 02
    10 h

    Why this comes next: Every cert on this list assumes you can read a packet and live in a terminal. CompTIA officially recommends Network+ before Security+, and Bandit makes the command line second nature before a hands-on exam ever tests it.

    • Professor Messer Network+Course · Beginner · Freemium↗
    • freeCodeCamp Computer Networking Full CourseCourse · Beginner · Free↗
    • OverTheWire BanditCTF Platform · Beginner · Free↗
  3. 03
    12 h

    Why this comes next: Security+ is the most requested cert in job listings and a common filter for government and entry roles. It is theory-heavy, but Professor Messer's free course keeps your prep cost at zero; the exam itself costs about $425 in 2026.

    • CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)Certification · Beginner · Paid↗
    • Professor Messer Security+ SY0-701 Coursevideo · Beginner · Freemium↗
  4. 04
    8 h

    Why this comes next: After Security+ theory, eJPT proves you can actually do the work: it is an open-book, hands-on exam over 48 hours, and at about $200 it is the cheapest practical credential that still means something on a junior resume.

    • INE eJPTCertification · Beginner · Paid↗
    • TCM Security Practical Ethical HackingCourse · Beginner · Paid↗
  5. 05
    8 h

    Why this comes next: PJPT gives you two days on a real internal network to compromise the domain controller, then two more to write a professional report, which is half of the actual pentester job. At $249 with the training course included, it is the best-value practical exam at this level.

    • TCM Security PJPTCertification · Beginner · Paid↗
    • TCM Security Practical Ethical HackingCourse · Beginner · Paid↗
  6. 06
    40 h

    Why this comes next: OSCP is the cert interviewers actually ask about: 24 hours of live exploitation plus a written report. Take it after the junior practicals above, because at $1,749 for the course, lab time, and one attempt, you do not want to walk in underprepared.

    • OffSec OSCP (PEN-200)Certification · Intermediate · Paid↗
    • HackTheBoxCTF Platform · Intermediate · Paid↗
  7. 07
    20 h

    Why this comes next: Past OSCP, generic certs stop paying off. The right specialist cert talks directly to the job: CRTO for red team, BTL1 for blue team, the AWS Security Specialty for cloud, or PNPT if you want a harder network pentest credential.

    • Zero-Point Security CRTOCertification · Advanced · Paid↗
    • Security Blue Team - BTL1Certification · Beginner · Paid↗
    • AWS Certified Security - Specialty (SCS-C03)Certification · Advanced · Paid↗
    • TCM Security PNPTCertification · Intermediate · Paid↗
  8. 08
    10 h

    Why this comes next: CISSP is a management-level cert that expects about five years of experience and costs $749, so it belongs at the end of this path, not the start. Until then, regular lab time and CTFs keep your skills sharper than collecting more exams.

    • ISC2 CISSPCertification · Advanced · Paid↗
    • CTFtimeCTF Platform · All Levels · Free↗
