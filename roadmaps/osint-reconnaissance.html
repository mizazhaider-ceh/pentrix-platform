Roadmap

OSINT & Reconnaissance

From Google dorking to breach data to reports a client would actually pay for, all without touching the target.

Audience
Beginners who want to gather intelligence legally and feed it into pentests or investigations
Difficulty
Beginner to Intermediate
Length
8-14 weeks
Steps
8 steps

The sequence

0 of 8 steps

Tick a step when you finish it. Progress is saved in this browser only.

  1. 01
    6 h

    Why this comes next: OSINT starts with knowing where the legal and ethical lines are: passive collection leaves no trace, active touches the target. Get this wrong and a fun exercise becomes a crime, so it comes before any tool.

    • OSINT FrameworkCheatsheet · Beginner · Free↗
    • OSINT Techniques (Michael Bazzell)Book · All Levels · Paid↗
  2. 02
    8 h

    Why this comes next: Search engines are the fastest OSINT tool you own and completely passive. A handful of operators pulls exposed files, login portals, and credentials out of the open web without you ever touching a target server.

    • Google Hacking Databasecheatsheet · Beginner · Free↗
    • OSINT Techniques (Michael Bazzell)Book · All Levels · Paid↗
  3. 03
    10 h

    Why this comes next: Every engagement starts with knowing what exists. Certificate Transparency logs reveal subdomains nobody would guess, and theHarvester automates the first sweep across public sources.

    • theHarvesterTool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • crt.shtool · Beginner · Free↗
    • OSINT FrameworkCheatsheet · Beginner · Free↗
  4. 04
    8 h

    Why this comes next: People reuse usernames everywhere. Sherlock checks hundreds of sites for one handle, and Epieos ties emails to linked accounts, turning a single name into a full profile.

    • SherlockTool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • WhatsMyNameTool · Beginner · Free↗
    • EpieosTool · Beginner · Freemium↗
  5. 05
    6 h

    Why this comes next: Breaches are where recon meets real risk: Have I Been Pwned tells you what is already public about a target, and this step teaches you to handle found credentials responsibly instead of trying them.

    • Have I Been Pwnedtool · Beginner · Free↗
    • OSINT Techniques (Michael Bazzell)Book · All Levels · Paid↗
  6. 06
    10 h

    Why this comes next: Posts carry timelines and locations; photos carry backgrounds worth geolocating. Bellingcat's published methods show you how to turn a public post into a verified fact, one screenshot at a time.

    • Bellingcat Resources and Toolkitsguide · Beginner · Free↗
    • OSINT FrameworkCheatsheet · Beginner · Free↗
  7. 07
    10 h

    Why this comes next: Subdomains tell you what exists; Shodan tells you what is running and exposed, and the Wayback Machine shows what used to be there. Together they fill the gaps that passive DNS misses.

    • Shodantool · Intermediate · Freemium↗
    • Wayback Machinetool · Beginner · Free↗
    • OSINT FrameworkCheatsheet · Beginner · Free↗
  8. 08
    12 h

    Why this comes next: Findings that are not documented did not happen. A report with sources and screenshots that anyone can reproduce is what separates a hobbyist from someone a team, a bounty triager, or a client trusts.

    • Trace Labs Search Party CTFCTF Platform · Beginner · Free↗
    • Bellingcat Resources and Toolkitsguide · Beginner · Free↗
    • OSINT Techniques (Michael Bazzell)Book · All Levels · Paid↗
