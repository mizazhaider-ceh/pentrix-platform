Roadmap

SOC Analyst Foundations

Everything a Tier-1 SOC analyst needs: networking, Linux, ATT&CK, triage, DFIR, and detection.

Audience
Career switchers aiming at a Tier-1 SOC role
Difficulty
Beginner to Intermediate
Length
12-20 weeks
Steps
8 steps

The sequence

0 of 8 steps

Tick a step when you finish it. Progress is saved in this browser only.

  1. 01
    10 h

    Why this comes next: SOC work is reading packets and logs; you need to recognize normal before you can spot abnormal.

    • freeCodeCamp Computer Networking Full CourseCourse · Beginner · Free↗
    • Professor Messer Network+Course · Beginner · Freemium↗
    • SubnettingPractice.comInteractive Lab · Beginner · Free↗
  2. 02
    8 h

    Why this comes next: Endpoints are Linux and alerts come with command lines; Bandit makes the terminal second nature.

    • OverTheWire BanditCTF Platform · Beginner · Free↗
    • Linux JourneyCourse · Beginner · Free↗
    • The Linux Command LineBook · Beginner · Free↗
  3. 03
    8 h

    Why this comes next: ATT&CK is the shared language every SOC uses to describe attacks; learn it before you triage your first alert.

    • MITRE ATT&CKCheatsheet · All Levels · Free↗
    • SANS DFIR PostersCheatsheet · All Levels · Free↗
    • Darknet DiariesPodcast · All Levels · Free↗
  4. 04
    15 h

    Why this comes next: Alert triage is the Tier-1 job; LetsDefend's alert queue and the BOTS datasets teach the workflow on realistic data.

    • LetsDefendInteractive Lab · Beginner · Freemium↗
    • Splunk BOTS (Boss of the SOC)CTF Platform · Beginner · Free↗
    • SANS Internet Storm CenterNewsletter · All Levels · Free↗
  5. 05
    20 h

    Why this comes next: Alerts become investigations; BTLO and CyberDefenders hand you real artifacts and ask the questions a real analyst answers.

    • Blue Team Labs OnlineInteractive Lab · Beginner · Freemium↗
    • CyberDefendersCTF Platform · Intermediate · Freemium↗
    • MemLabsCTF Platform · Intermediate · Free↗
  6. 06
    12 h

    Why this comes next: Once you have triaged attacks, learn to write the detections yourself: Sigma rules are the core detection-as-code skill.

    • Sigma rules repositoryCheatsheet · Intermediate · Free↗
    • Splunk BOTS (Boss of the SOC)CTF Platform · Beginner · Free↗
    • VelociraptorTool · Intermediate · Free↗
  7. 07
    12 h

    Why this comes next: A Wazuh homelab gives you a SIEM to practice in daily and a story to tell in interviews.

    • WazuhTool · Intermediate · Free↗
    • SANS DFIR PostersCheatsheet · All Levels · Free↗
    • John Hammond (YouTube)YouTube Channel · Beginner · Free↗

    40 h

    Why this comes next: BTL1 is the hands-on blue-team credential hiring managers recognize; the DFIR Report write-ups show you what exam-level analysis looks like.

    • The DFIR ReportNewsletter · Intermediate · Free↗
