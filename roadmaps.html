How to use a roadmap

  1. 01

    Pick one path

    Start from the roadmap that matches your level today, not the role you want in a year. One path finished beats three started.

  2. 02

    Do one step a week

    Every step shows its time. Block the hours, finish each resource in the step, then move on. One step a week is the default pace.

  3. 03

    Check the why-next note before skipping

    Every step carries a gold note explaining why it comes next. Read it before you jump ahead. It tells you what the next step assumes you already know.
